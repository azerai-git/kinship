Kinship works with Firefox Sync

Your Mozilla account session ended, so syncing is paused.

Sign in with your Mozilla account

Use the account you use for Firefox Sync. You'll sign in on Mozilla's own page (accounts.firefox.com), in a new tab, including two-step authentication. Kinship never sees your password.

Choose what to sync

Everything is encrypted on this device with your account's Sync key before it's uploaded, and goes only to Mozilla's servers, the same as Firefox. Kinship has no servers of its own, and its developer never receives your data.

Your browser will ask permission for the options that need it. You can change these later in Settings.

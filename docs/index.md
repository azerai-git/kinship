# Kinship – works with Firefox Sync

An unofficial, open-source extension for Chrome and Edge: your Firefox bookmarks,
synced tabs, history and Send Tab, end-to-end encrypted with your own Mozilla
account. No servers of its own.

- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)
